Having a professional team deep clean your home gives you an amazing fresh start. The key to maintaining that pristine feeling all month long is building a handful of effortless daily micro-habits.

### 1. The 2-Minute Kitchen Reset

Wiping kitchen counters and clearing the sink every evening prevents grime from settling into grout and porous surfaces. A quick spritz of warm water and microfiber cloth keeps stainless steel and stone looking polished.

### 2. High-Traffic Floor Care

Entryways and living spaces collect 80% of outdoor dust. Placing durable entrance mats inside and outside your doorways dramatically reduces the amount of dirt tracked onto hardwood and carpets.

### 3. Squeegee Shower Glass After Use

Hard water spots take hours to scrub away once mineral deposits dry. Keeping a small silicone squeegee in your shower and taking 30 seconds to wipe the glass cuts bathroom maintenance time in half.
